About CIVCS-2027

The International Conference on Computational Intelligence in VLSI Design, Communication, and Signal Processing (CIVCS-2027) aims to bring together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and students working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, semiconductor technology, communication systems, signal processing and intelligent cyber-physical systems.
