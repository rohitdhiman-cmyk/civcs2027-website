Welcome to CIVCS-2027

The International Conference on Computational Intelligence in VLSI Design, Communication and Signal Processing (CIVCS-2027), to be held at National Institute of Technology Hamirpur, India during May 21–23, 2027, aims to bring together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and students working at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, semiconductor technology, communication systems, signal processing, and intelligent cyber-physical systems.

The conference seeks to provide an international platform for dissemination of cutting-edge research in AI-enabled VLSI design, next-generation communication systems, intelligent biomedical signal processing, emerging devices, nanotechnology, IoT and smart sensing systems.

Conference Tracks

  1. AI in VLSI Design
    Machine Learning for EDA, Hardware Accelerators, PPA Optimisation, Hardware Security, Low-Power Circuit Design.

  2. Next-Generation Communication Systems
    AI-enabled 5G/6G Networks, Massive MIMO, Network Slicing, THz Communication Systems and ML-based Channel Estimation.

  3. Intelligent Signal Processing
    Biomedical Signal Analysis, Medical Imaging, Adaptive Signal Processing and Graph Neural Networks.

  4. Intelligent Cyber-Physical Systems and IoT
    Smart Cities, Industrial IoT, Autonomous Vehicles and Federated Learning for IoT.

  5. Emerging Devices and Nanotechnology
    Spintronics, Neuromorphic Computing, 2D Materials and Quantum Dot Device Simulation.

Publication

Accepted and presented papers are planned to be published in Springer Proceedings (subject to approval) following a rigorous peer-review process.

Paper Submission

Authors are invited to submit original, unpublished manuscripts through Microsoft CMT. All submissions will undergo double-blind peer review with at least two independent reviewers.
